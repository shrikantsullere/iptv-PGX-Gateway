# PGX Gateway - Software Flow & User Guide

Welcome to the **PGX Gateway** platform! This guide will walk you through how our software works and how you can use it to manage your payments, customers, and revenues step-by-step.

---

## 🚀 How the System Works (The Big Picture)
PGX Gateway is designed to be your central hub for processing and managing online payments. 

**The Core Flow:**
1. **Customer Buys:** A customer makes a purchase on your website.
2. **Transaction Clears:** The gateway securely processes the payment and blocks any fraudulent attempts.
3. **Funds Added:** The money (minus the gateway processing fee) is instantly deposited into your digital **Wallet** on the platform.
4. **Data Updated:** The customer's details and your total revenue charts are automatically updated in real-time.
5. **Withdrawal:** You request a payout to your bank account via the **Settlement Center**.

---

## 💻 Step-by-Step Guide to Using the Platform

### 1. Dashboard (Your Command Center)
* **What it is:** The first screen you see when you log in.
* **How to use it:** Use this page to get a quick snapshot of your business health. You will see your Total Available Balance, Pending Settlements, and a list of your most recent transactions.

### 2. Transactions
* **What it is:** A complete history of every payment made through your gateway.
* **How to use it:** If a customer asks about a payment, come here. You can search by transaction ID, filter by "Completed" or "Failed", and see exactly why a payment might have declined.

### 3. Wallet Management
* **What it is:** Your digital ledger.
* **How to use it:** Check this page to see exactly how much money you can withdraw right now. It shows your Available Balance, Total Deposits, and any funds that might be temporarily frozen due to disputes.

### 4. Settlement Center (Getting Paid)
* **What it is:** The place where you withdraw your money to your real bank account.
* **How to use it:** 
  1. Click on **"Linked Accounts"** to securely add your Bank Account or Crypto Wallet.
  2. Once linked, go to **"Request Payout"**.
  3. Enter the amount you want to withdraw and hit submit. The admin team will process your payout and wire the money to you!

### 5. Customers (CRM)
* **What it is:** Your customer database.
* **How to use it:** The system automatically saves everyone who pays you. You can see their "Lifetime Value" (how much they've spent with you in total) and their risk profile. You can also manually **Add, Edit, or Delete** customer profiles or download the entire list.

### 6. Revenue Analytics
* **What it is:** Your financial performance tracker.
* **How to use it:** View beautifully designed charts that show your Gross Revenue versus the Gateway Fees you've paid over time. Need a report for your accountant? Just click the **"Download Statement"** button to instantly generate an Excel (CSV) file of your earnings.

## 🔒 Security & Peace of Mind
Behind the scenes, the **Super Admin** team is constantly monitoring the health of the payment processors and tracking the "Security Operations Center" (SOC). If someone tries to use stolen cards or perform chargeback fraud, the system flags and blocks them, keeping your funds safe and secure.
